Meet the Owner

Dr. Sumit Sharma

General Dentist & Clinic Owner

Dr. Sumit Sharma is an experienced and compassionate dentist with an extensive clinical background spanning both India and Canada. He practiced dentistry in India from 2008 to 2019, gaining broad experience across various aspects of dental care. After moving to Canada, he successfully completed the Canadian dental equivalency process in 2023 and has since been practicing dentistry in Vancouver.

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Sharma has served as a Clinical Instructor at the University of British Columbia (UBC), providing clinical guidance to dental students. He is also passionate about mentorship and has supported internationally trained dentists preparing for board and qualifying examinations across Canada and the United States.

Dr. Sharma believes that exceptional dentistry goes beyond treating teeth — it is about building trust, understanding each patient's individual needs, and creating a comfortable and positive experience. His approach combines extensive clinical experience with personalised attention, clear communication, and a genuine commitment to helping patients feel confident about their oral health and their smiles.
